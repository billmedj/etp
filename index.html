A transaction boundary for autonomous effects.

ETP binds one proposed external action to explicit authority, current target state, single-use execution, and durable outcome evidence.

Failure semantics

An executor consumes a grant before dispatch. An unclear result becomes an unknown outcome. Reconciliation adds evidence without restoring authority.
