# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting different opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing to affected people, and learning from
  mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
They will take fair corrective action in response to behavior that they consider
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders can remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki
edits, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.
They will give a reason for a moderation decision when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces. It also applies when a
person officially represents the community in a public space.

## Enforcement

Use the private contact method on the
[project lead's GitHub profile](https://github.com/billmedj) to report abusive,
harassing, or unacceptable behavior. If no private contact method is available,
open a minimal issue that requests a private channel. Do not include sensitive
details in the issue.

Project leaders must protect the privacy and security of a reporter.

## Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders will use these guidelines when they determine the consequence
of a violation.

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:** Inappropriate language or other conduct that is
unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** A private written warning. The warning explains the violation
and why the behavior was inappropriate. Community leaders can request a public
apology.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:** A violation through one incident or a series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning that states the consequences of continued behavior.
The person must not interact with the affected people or enforcement team for a
specified period. A further violation can result in a temporary or permanent
ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Community impact:** A serious violation, including sustained inappropriate
behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from community interaction for a specified
period. The person must not contact the affected people or enforcement team
during that period. A further violation can result in a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Community impact:** A pattern of violations, harassment, aggression, or
disparagement of a class of people.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from public community interaction.
